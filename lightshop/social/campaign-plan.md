# Social media plan: first 90 days, then launch

**Goal:** build an audience *before* the shop opens, so launch day already has buyers. Your budget is 5 h a week for content, so everything below is batched and repeatable.

## Channels, in priority order

| Channel | Role | Why for lamps | Posts / week |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (Reels + carousels) | Main channel, shop on Instagram, DMs for B2B | Swiss design audience, very visual product | 3 Reels + 1 carousel + daily Stories |
| **TikTok** | Reach | Print time-lapses go viral; "satisfying" 3D printing is a big niche | Same 3 Reels, reposted |
| **Pinterest** | Long-term traffic and SEO | Interior inspiration, pins bring clicks for years | 10 pins / week (scheduled, 30 min) |
| LinkedIn | B2B only | Architects, restaurant owners, hotels | 1 / week from month 3 |
| YouTube Shorts | Free extra reach | Same video files | Repost |

## Positioning in one line

**"Lampen aus Schichten: entworfen, gedruckt und von Hand montiert in der Schweiz."** ("Lamps made of layers: designed, printed and assembled by hand in Switzerland.")
Show the process openly. It's your advantage over imported lamps and over hobby printers.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Making of** (process, satisfying) | 40% | Print time-lapse → lamp switched on in the dark. Layer close-ups. "1,333 layers in 9 hours". Removing the print from the plate. Assembly in 30 seconds |
| **2. Light in rooms** (desire) | 25% | Same lamp: day vs night. Lamp in a real Zürich flat. Föhn on a balcony at sunset. Firn over a dining table |
| **3. Design story** (brand) | 15% | Why it's called Föhn. Sketch → prototype → final. Failed prints ("Prototyp 7 von 12") |
| **4. Knowledge** (trust, SEO) | 10% | "Which bulb in a 3D-printed lamp?" "2700 K explained in 15 seconds". Safety test with the thermometer |
| **5. People** (social proof) | 10% | Customer photos, cafés using your lamps, packing orders, behind the scenes |

## Video formats that work for printed lamps (tested patterns)

1. **"Watch it grow"**: 9 h time-lapse in 8 s, hard cut to the lit lamp in a dark room. Text: "9 Stunden. 1'333 Schichten. 1 Lampe."
2. **"Guess the time"**: show the finished lamp, ask "Wie lange hat der Druck gedauert?", answer in the comments. Drives engagement.
3. **"Day / Night"**: the same frame, switch on. Lamps sell at night.
4. **"Prototype graveyard"**: 12 failed versions on a table. Shows craft and is relatable.
5. **"Pack an order with me"**: packing ASMR, the handwritten layer-count card. Shows the human behind the brand.
6. **"Colour roulette"**: the AMS spins, followers vote on the next colour.
7. **"Swiss names"**: "Föhn is a warm wind in the Alps…", plus a landscape shot.

**Filming setup:** the Bambu printers' built-in camera works for time-lapses. Add a phone tripod, one dark corner with a wooden table or concrete wall, and an extra 2700 K bulb. Film on Sundays: 2 h gives 6–8 clips.

## Timeline

### Phase 0: Set-up (week 1)
- Reserve handles on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest (same name as the domain).
- Instagram: switch to a business account. Link in bio → waitlist page (Shopify password page with email sign-up).
- Write the bio: "Lampen aus Schichten. Gedruckt & montiert in der Schweiz 🇨🇭 · Launch [Datum] · Warteliste ↓" ("Lamps made of layers. Printed & assembled in Switzerland · launch [date] · waitlist ↓")
- Build 3 highlight covers: Making of · Lampen (lamps) · FAQ.

### Phase 1: Build in public (weeks 2–8)
- Topic: "I'm starting a lamp studio". Real, imperfect, the process.
- 3 Reels a week (pillars 1 + 3), always ending with "Warteliste in der Bio" (waitlist in bio).
- **Engage for 15 min a day:** comment properly on Swiss interior accounts, cafés and designers. This is the cheapest growth there is.
- Week 6: **vote on the next lamp colour**, which builds involvement.
- Target by week 8: **500–1,000 followers, 150+ people on the waitlist.**

### Phase 2: Pre-launch (weeks 9–11)
- Announce the launch date. Countdown Stories.
- Seed **5–8 micro-creators** (Swiss interior and lifestyle, 3k–30k followers). Give each a lamp for an honest post, with no obligation. Cost: about 6 × CHF 40 in materials. Find them by searching #wohnenschweiz, #interiorzurich, #schweizerdesign, #altbauliebe.
- Offer **3 cafés or restaurants** a free trial set of Föhn for 4 weeks in exchange for a photo shoot and credit.
- Waitlist email: "Du bekommst 48 h Vorsprung und eine handschriftliche Karte." ("You get 48 hours' head start and a handwritten card.") Use exclusivity, not a discount.

### Phase 3: Launch (week 12)
- Day 1: launch Reel (the best time-lapse), email to the waitlist, all Stories.
- Days 2–7: one product per day, plus creators' posts as Stories.
- **Limited first run:** "Erste Serie: 50 nummerierte Lampen" ("First run: 50 numbered lamps"). Numbering fits the layer-count card, creates urgency without discounts, and protects your 15 h a week.

### Phase 4: After launch (months 4–6)
- Collect reviews (Judge.me email 14 days after delivery) and repost customer photos.
- Start the **paid test** once you have 20 sales: CHF 10–15 a day on Meta, 3 weeks (see the market report). Use the 3 best organic Reels as ads.
- Start B2B on LinkedIn: one restaurant case study a month.
- One new lamp a month = one launch moment a month.
