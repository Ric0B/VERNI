# First 4 weeks: every post written out

Phase 1, "build in public". 3 Reels + 1 carousel a week, Stories daily, 10 pins a week. Every Reel goes to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Captions are in German (main market) with a short English line for reach.

**Filming kit:** phone on a tripod, the Bambu camera time-lapse, one dark corner with a concrete or wood surface, one extra 2700 K bulb for fill. Film everything for a week on one Sunday (2 h).

**Format rules:** 7–15 s. Text on screen in the first 0.5 s. No music with lyrics. Natural sound of the printer is part of the brand. End on the lamp switched on.

---

## Week 1: "Ich gründe ein Lampen-Atelier" (I'm starting a lamp studio)

### Reel 1 · Mon · "9 Stunden in 9 Sekunden" (9 hours in 9 seconds)
- **Hook text (0.0 s):** „9 Stunden. 1'333 Schichten." ("9 hours. 1,333 layers.")
- **Shots:** Bambu time-lapse of Aare (8 s) → hard cut: hand lifts the shade off the plate (1 s) → dark room, click, the lamp lights up (2 s, hold).
- **Sound:** real printer sound, the click of the switch.
- **Caption:** „Das ist die erste Lampe aus unserem Atelier. Eine einzige Linie, die sich 1'333 Mal um sich selbst windet. Gedruckt über Nacht, montiert von Hand, in der Schweiz. Bald mehr. 🇨🇭 / Our first lamp: one single line, 1,333 layers."
  ("This is the first lamp from our studio. A single line that winds round itself 1,333 times. Printed overnight, assembled by hand, in Switzerland. More soon.")
- **Hashtags:** #3dgedruckt #schweizerdesign #designlampe #bambulab #madeinswitzerland

### Reel 2 · Wed · "Berechnet, nicht gezeichnet" (Computed, not drawn)
- **Hook:** „Diese Lampe ist eine Formel." ("This lamp is a formula.")
- **Shots:** screen recording of the Formlabor, moving the "Rippen" (ribs) slider from 12 to 96 (4 s) → "Drehung" (twist) slider (3 s) → the printed result on the table, turning on a lazy Susan (4 s).
- **Caption:** „Wir zeichnen unsere Lampen nicht, wir rechnen sie. Ein paar Zahlen, Rippen, Drehung, Tiefe, und es entsteht eine neue Form. Welche Version würdest du drucken? / We don't draw our lamps, we compute them."
  ("We don't draw our lamps, we compute them. A few numbers, ribs, twist, depth, and a new form appears. Which version would you print?")
- **Hashtags:** #parametricdesign #computationaldesign #grasshopper3d #3dprinting #lightingdesign

### Carousel 1 · Thu · "Wer wir sind" (Who we are; 6 slides)
1. Photo of you at the printers. Text: „Hallo. Wir machen Lampen aus Schichten." ("Hello. We make lamps out of layers.")
2. Close-up of layer lines. „Jede Schicht ist 0.24 mm dünn." ("Each layer is 0.24 mm thin.")
3. Sketch or Formlabor screenshot. „Jede Form ist eine Formel." ("Every form is a formula.")
4. Hands fitting the socket. „Elektrik nur aus zertifizierten Teilen." ("Electrics only from certified parts.")
5. The layer-count card. „Jede Lampe bekommt ihre Schichtzahl." ("Every lamp gets its layer count.")
6. Lit lamp. „Launch im Frühling. Warteliste in der Bio." ("Launch in spring. Waitlist in bio.")
- **Caption:** „Ein kleines Atelier, zwei Drucker, viele Prototypen. Folge mit, wie es entsteht." ("A small studio, two printers, many prototypes. Follow along as it comes together.")

### Reel 3 · Sat · "Prototyp 7 von 12" (Prototype 7 of 12)
- **Hook:** „12 Versuche für eine Lampe." ("12 attempts for one lamp.")
- **Shots:** 12 failed prototypes in a row on a table, camera tracks along (5 s) → stops on the final one, which lights up (3 s).
- **Caption:** „Zu tief, zu flach, zu streifig, umgekippt, zu warm… Nummer 12 ist die Aare. Was ist dein liebster Fehlversuch?" ("Too deep, too flat, too stripy, tipped over, too warm… Number 12 is the Aare. Which failed attempt is your favourite?")
- **Engagement:** pin a comment: "Nummer 4 hätte ich fast behalten 👀" ("I almost kept number 4").

**Stories this week:** a poll ("Licht an oder aus: was ist schöner?" / "Light on or off: which is nicer?"), the printer at 2 am, packing the first test lamp, a "Frag mich etwas" (ask me anything) box.

## Week 2: Material and light

### Reel 4 · Mon · "Tag / Nacht" (Day / night)
- **Hook:** „Ausgeschaltet: Keramik? Eingeschaltet:" ("Switched off: ceramic? Switched on:")
- **Shots:** locked camera, Gletscher on a sideboard in daylight (3 s) → crossfade to the same frame at night, lit (4 s).
- **Caption:** „Tagsüber ein Objekt, abends ein Licht. Gletscher, unser erstes Unikat: Jedes Muster gibt es nur einmal." ("An object by day, a light by evening. Gletscher, our first one-off: every pattern exists only once.")

### Reel 5 · Wed · "Warum PETG an der Fassung" (Why PETG at the socket)
- **Hook:** „Darf man in 3D-gedruckte Lampen eine Glühbirne schrauben? Nein." ("Can you put an incandescent bulb in a 3D-printed lamp? No.")
- **Shots:** IR thermometer on the shade during the 4-hour test, numbers visible (5 s) → close-up of the PETG mount (2 s) → LED bulb box "4.9 W" (2 s).
- **Caption:** „PLA wird ab 55 °C weich. Darum: nur LED bis 7 W, Abstand zur Birne, PETG an der Fassung, und jede Form 4 Stunden mit dem Thermometer getestet. Sicherheit ist bei uns kein Kleingedrucktes." ("PLA softens from 55 °C. So: LED only up to 7 W, distance from the bulb, PETG at the socket, and every form tested for 4 hours with a thermometer. Safety isn't small print for us.")
- **Hashtags:** #3ddruck #pla #lampensicherheit #maker

### Carousel 2 · Thu · "Lichtfarbe in 5 Slides" (Light colour in 5 slides)
Slides: 2200 K · 2700 K · 3000 K · 4000 K (same lamp photographed under each, with the room it suits) · „Wir liefern 2700 K mit." ("We include 2700 K.") Link to the journal article.

### Reel 6 · Sat · "Farbroulette" (Colour roulette)
- **Hook:** „Welche Farbe drucken wir als nächstes?" ("Which colour do we print next?")
- **Shots:** the AMS spinning through 4 spools (4 s) → 4 small Glut lamps in the colours (3 s).
- **CTA:** vote in Stories. The winning colour gets printed next week (and filmed).

**Stories:** the colour vote, behind the scenes of filament drying, a question box "Was willst du über 3D-Druck wissen?" ("What do you want to know about 3D printing?")

## Week 3: One-offs and the Formlabor

### Reel 7 · Mon · "Dein Datum als Lampe" (Your date as a lamp)
- **Hook:** „Dieses Muster ist ein Hochzeitsdatum." ("This pattern is a wedding date.")
- **Shots:** typing "14062019" into the Formlabor code field, the pattern changes (4 s) → the printed lamp with the certificate beside it (4 s).
- **Caption:** „Bei der Gletscher-Lampe bestimmt eine Zahl die Spalten. Wir drucken jeden Code genau einmal. Ein Geburtstag, ein Hochzeitstag, eine Postleitzahl: Welche Zahl wäre deine?" ("In the Gletscher lamp, one number decides the crevasses. We print each code exactly once. A birthday, a wedding anniversary, a postcode: what would your number be?")

### Reel 8 · Wed · "Drei Codes, drei Lampen" (Three codes, three lamps)
- **Shots:** three Gletscher lamps side by side, lit one after another with their codes as on-screen text.
- **Caption:** „Code 23, 42, 108. Gleiche Formel, drei Unikate." ("Codes 23, 42, 108. Same formula, three one-offs.")

### Carousel 3 · Thu · "Was ist ein Attraktor?" (What is an attractor?)
Explains the Moräne in 6 slides: straight ribs → one invisible point → ribs swerve → four points → the printed lamp → „Edition von 50" ("Edition of 50"). Designers and architects save these.

### Reel 9 · Sat · the colour vote result being printed (time-lapse) + reveal.

**Stories:** your own code request ("Schick mir deine Zahl, ich zeige dir dein Muster" / "Send me your number and I'll show you your pattern"). Reply with Formlabor screenshots. This builds the one-off waitlist.

## Week 4: Behind the brand

### Reel 10 · Mon · "Warum Föhn?" (Why Föhn?)
- **Hook:** „Wir benennen Lampen nach Schweizer Wetter." ("We name lamps after Swiss weather.")
- **Shots:** windy Alpine footage (your own, or film a lake on a windy day) → Föhn on a balcony at dusk.

### Reel 11 · Wed · "Montage in 30 Sekunden" (Assembly in 30 seconds)
- Hands: tube into base, nut, socket, shade drops into the groove, bulb, click. Satisfying, no talking.
- **Caption:** „Kein Kleber, keine Schrauben am Schirm. Alles lässt sich tauschen." ("No glue, no screws on the shade. Everything can be swapped.")

### Carousel 4 · Thu · "Launch-Datum" (Launch date)
Countdown begins: the date, what's in the first series (50 numbered lamps), how the waitlist gets 48 hours' head start.

### Reel 12 · Sat · "Der ganze Weg" (The whole journey)
A 15-second montage of the month: formula → print → fails → test → finished lamps lit in a row.
