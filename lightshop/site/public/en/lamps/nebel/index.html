Floor lamp

Nebel

A column of light, like morning fog over the lake.

CHF 349

Colour
  • Printed and assembled in Switzerland
  • Free shipping in Switzerland over CHF 120
  • 30-day returns
  • 2-year warranty, spare parts for life

Anyone who lives on the Swiss plateau knows it: the low fog that sits for days in November. The Nebel floor lamp takes its light and leaves the gloom.

Four segments, 21 hours of printing and 5,000 layers in total, sit on an aluminium tube. Two bulbs light the whole column evenly.

It ships in parts and goes together in five minutes without tools. If anything happens, we replace a single segment.

  • Quick guide and safety notes

Care

Dust with a dry microfibre cloth or soft brush. No water, no solvents. Keep away from direct sun behind glass and from radiators. Use LED bulbs up to 7 W only.
