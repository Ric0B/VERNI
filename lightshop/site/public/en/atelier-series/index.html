Atelier series

Shapes you cannot draw, only compute. Every Atelier lamp grows from a formula with a few parameters: number of ribs, twist, attractor points, a random code. We print them in small editions or as one-offs, with a Swiss oak base and a dimmer.

The method

Parameters

Every shape is an equation for the radius as a function of height and angle. A handful of numbers control everything.

Printability

Before a shape is released, the program checks every wall for its angle. Nothing may overhang more steeply than 50 degrees.

One line

It prints in spiral mode: a single, unbroken line from bottom to top, without a seam.

Signed

Editions carry their number in the wooden base. One-offs come with a certificate and their code.
