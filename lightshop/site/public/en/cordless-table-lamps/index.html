Frequently asked questions

How long does the battery last?

Föhn about 12 hours at medium brightness, Glut about 20 hours on low. A full charge takes about 4 hours.

Can I use them outdoors?

On a covered balcony or terrace, yes. Please bring them in when it rains: they are not waterproof.

Can the battery be replaced?

Yes. The light unit is screwed in and we can swap it.
