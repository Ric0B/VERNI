Bedside lamp: how bright, how warm, how big?

Bright enough to read, warm enough to fall asleep: how to choose a bedside lamp, with lumens, kelvin and height.

A bedside lamp has two jobs that contradict each other: give enough light to read, and still not keep you awake in the evening.

Brightness: 250 to 400 lumens, ideally dimmable

250 to 400 lumens is enough to read when the lamp stands close to the bed. A dimmer is worth more than extra brightness: bright to read, very low to fall asleep.

Light colour: 2200 to 2700 kelvin

The warmer the light in the evening, the less it disturbs your sleep rhythm. 2700 K is a good compromise for reading. For the last light before sleep, 2200 K, candlelight, is ideal. That is why our Glut night light glows at exactly that.

Size: bottom of the shade at shoulder height when sitting

Sitting up in bed, the bottom edge of the shade should be at about shoulder height. The light then falls on the book, not into your eyes. On a 55 cm bedside table, lamps 30 to 45 cm tall fit.

Cordless is practical

Not every bedside table is next to a socket. Rechargeable lamps like Föhn or Glut are charged once a week and stand wherever they are needed.
