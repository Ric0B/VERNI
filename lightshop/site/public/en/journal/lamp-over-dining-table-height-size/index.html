The lamp over the dining table: height, size, how many

How high should a pendant hang over the dining table, how big should it be, and when do you need two or three? The rules of thumb, with numbers.

Over the dining table, the lamp decides whether dinner feels cosy or like a canteen. Three numbers help.

Height: 60 to 75 cm above the table top

The bottom edge of the lamp should hang 60 to 75 cm above the table. Lower and it blocks the view across the table; higher and the bulb dazzles the people sitting there. With high ceilings these values still apply: the lamp belongs to the table, not the ceiling.

Size: a third to half of the table width

A table 90 cm wide takes a shade of 30 to 45 cm. Our Firn is 34.6 cm across, which suits most tables for four to six people.

How many: two or three from 160 cm length

  • Up to 160 cm long: one lamp, centred.
  • 160 to 240 cm: two lamps, each a quarter of the length from the end.
  • Over 240 cm: three lamps, evenly spaced, at least one shade diameter apart.

Light: warm and glare-free

600 to 800 lumens at 2700 kelvin is right over a dining table. More important than brightness is that nobody looks straight into the bulb. A shade that is open at the bottom and diffuses sideways softens faces and makes food look good.
