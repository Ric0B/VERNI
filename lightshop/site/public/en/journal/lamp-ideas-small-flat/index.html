5 lamp ideas for small flats

Little space, lots of atmosphere: five ways lamps make a small flat feel larger and more lived-in.

1. Light into the corners

Dark corners make a room feel smaller. A slim floor lamp in the corner pushes the walls outwards visually.

2. A lamp that is something when switched off

In small rooms every object counts. A lamp with form replaces a vase or sculpture during the day. That is why our lamps look like ceramics when they are off.

3. Narrow rather than wide

Tall, narrow lamps need little surface and lead the eye upwards. A diameter of 20 cm is enough for a table lamp that shapes a whole room.

4. One light for two zones

A pendant over the dining table separates the eating area from the living area without a wall. Light is the cheapest room divider.

5. Mobile rather than fixed

A rechargeable lamp moves with you: on the table for dinner, by the sofa afterwards, by the bed for reading. One lamp, three places.
