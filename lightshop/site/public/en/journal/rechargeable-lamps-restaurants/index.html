Rechargeable table lamps in restaurants: costs, routine, experience

Cordless table lamps instead of candles: what they cost, how long the battery lasts, and how charging works in service.

More and more restaurants and bars are replacing tea lights with cordless table lamps. We did the maths on whether it pays off.

The costs compared

A tea light costs about 10 centimes and burns for 4 hours. With 20 tables and 300 opening days, that is around CHF 600 a year, plus cleaning glasses and wax stains. 20 rechargeable lamps cost more once but last for years, and charging costs a few francs a year.

The routine

  • Evening: take the lamps from the charging station and hand them out; one tap switches them on.
  • Night: collect them and put them back in the station.
  • One charge lasts about 12 hours at medium brightness, so a whole evening with reserve.

What guests notice

Even, warm light without flicker and without smell. Faces look better, menus are readable. And a lamp with character becomes part of the concept, not just an accessory.

What to look for

  • A sturdy base that does not tip when someone bumps the table.
  • A replaceable battery.
  • A light colour of 2200 to 2700 K.
  • Replacements within days if a lamp falls.
