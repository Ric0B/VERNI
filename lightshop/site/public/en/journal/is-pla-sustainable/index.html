Is PLA sustainable? What plant-based plastic can and cannot do

PLA is made from plants, but is it compostable? A straight answer, and why repairability matters more than the material.

Where PLA comes from

PLA (polylactic acid) is made from sugar or starch from plants such as corn or sugar cane, not from oil. Producing it emits clearly less CO₂ than many conventional plastics.

Compostable? Only industrially

PLA only breaks down in industrial composting plants at high temperatures. In a garden compost or in nature it lasts for years. That is why we never call our lamps "biodegradable".

What really counts: using things for a long time

The most sustainable lamp is one that lasts twenty years. Our lamps are screwed together, not glued. Shades, segments and cables can be replaced individually. Tired of a colour? Swap only the shade.

Printed to order

We print in small batches and many colours only once they are ordered. There is no warehouse of lamps nobody wants, and very little transport: the lamps are made where they are sold.
