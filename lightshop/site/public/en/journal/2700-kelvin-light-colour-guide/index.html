2200, 2700 or 4000 kelvin? The right light colour for every room

Warm white, neutral white, daylight: which light colour suits the living room, bedroom, kitchen and office, explained simply.

Every LED box shows a number with K. It describes the colour of the light, not its brightness. The lower the number, the warmer and more golden the light.

The rule of thumb

  • 2200 K: like candlelight. For bedside, kids' rooms, evening mood.
  • 2700 K: classic warm white. For living rooms, dining tables, bedrooms.
  • 3000 K: a little fresher. For kitchens and bathrooms.
  • 4000 K: neutral white. For offices, workshops and work surfaces.

Why we include 2700 K

Printed shades tint the light slightly, depending on the colour of the material. A natural shade makes it warmer, a grey one a little cooler. At 2700 K each of our colours looks the way we designed it.

Brightness: lumens, not watts

A mood lamp needs 250 to 450 lumens. Over a dining table, 600 to 800 is right. Check the lumen value and the lamp's maximum wattage.
