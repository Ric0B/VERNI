Frequently asked questions

Are 3D printed lamps safe?

Yes, when they are built properly. We only use certified sockets, cables and plugs, print the part around the socket in heat-resistant PETG, and test every shape for four hours with the strongest LED we allow. Our lamps are designed for LED bulbs up to 7 W, never incandescent bulbs.

What are the lamps made of?

PLA, a plastic made from plant sources such as corn starch, and partly PETG. The electrics are certified parts from European manufacturers.

How long does delivery take?

Lamps in stock ship within 2 working days with Swiss Post. Colours we print to order take 5 to 7 working days.

Do you ship to Germany and Austria?

Not yet. We are working on it. Join the newsletter to hear first.

Can I order a custom colour or size?

Yes, from a single lamp. Tell us what you have in mind. Custom colours cost CHF 20 extra; size changes are quoted individually.

What if a part breaks?

Every lamp comes apart. Shades, segments and cables can be reordered individually for as long as we exist.

Which bulb should I use?

An LED with an E27 thread, 2700 kelvin, 7 watts at most. Every E27 lamp comes with a matching bulb.

Do you offer trade prices for restaurants and architects?

Yes, from 10 lamps. See Hospitality.
