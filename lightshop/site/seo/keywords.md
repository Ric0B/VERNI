## Off-page (links from other sites, the real ranking lever for a new domain)

- Google Business Profile for the workshop: shows in Maps and local search ("Lampen Zürich").
- Swiss directories: local.ch, search.ch, and Swiss design or maker directories.
- Pitch Swiss design and interior blogs and magazines: Wohnrevue, Das Ideale Heim, Schöner Wohnen Schweiz, Zürcher Wohnblogs.
- Every B2B customer: ask for a credit on their website ("Leuchten: Schichtlicht").
- Pinterest: every product image as a pin with a link. Pinterest search sends traffic for years.
- Apply for Swiss design awards and markets (Blickfang 2027, Design Preis Schweiz).

## After launch

1. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit `sitemap.xml`.
2. Set up Google Merchant Center for free product listings, fed by Shopify's Google & YouTube app.
3. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console.
4. Check every month: queries with high impressions and low CTR → rewrite those titles.
