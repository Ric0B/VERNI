# 4 · Brand name ideas

**Check before you decide.** This session couldn't reach domain registries, so none of the domains below are confirmed free. Takes 10 minutes:
1. Domain: search at [nic.ch](https://www.nic.ch) or a registrar (Hostpoint, Infomaniak, Cyon) for `.ch` and `.com`.
2. Company name: search [zefix.ch](https://www.zefix.ch) (commercial register).
3. Trademark: search [Swissreg](https://www.swissreg.ch) and [EUIPO eSearch](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) in class 11 (lamps).
4. Social handles: check Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.

Quick web searches found no lamp brand for *Glimm*, *Liechtli* or *Schichtlicht*. One near-clash: "Glimminge" is a lamp by the Swedish maker Konsthantverk.

## Shortlist

| Name | Meaning | + | – | Domains to check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Schichtlicht** ⭐ | "Layer light". Every lamp is built layer by layer | Tells the 3D-print story in one word. Works in Germany and Austria. Memorable | Harder for English speakers | schichtlicht.ch / .com |
| **Liechtli** | Swiss German for "little light" | Warm and unmistakably Swiss. Nobody uses it | Germans may misspell it. Sounds small rather than premium | liechtli.ch |
| **Glimm** | From "glimmen", to glow softly | Short, works in German and English | Close to "Glimminge"; ".com" probably taken | glimm.ch, glimm-studio.ch |
| **Atelier Faden** | "Thread", the filament | Crafted, premium | Generic | atelierfaden.ch |
| **Lumen Alpin** | Light + Alps | Clearly Swiss, international | A bit generic, close to many brands | lumenalpin.ch |
| **Schein** | Glow / appearance | One strong word | Also means "bill" and "fake" in German | scheinstudio.ch |
| **Firnlicht** | Firn = old snow on a glacier, which glows | Poetic and Swiss | Long | firnlicht.ch |

**My recommendation: Schichtlicht** (short form "SCHICHT" for the logo, with a ".ch" that fits neatly: schicht**licht.ch**). The draft website uses it as a working name. Changing it means editing `BRAND` in `site/content.py` and running `python3 build.py`.

Lamp names follow Swiss landscape words: Aare, Föhn, Firn, Grat, Nebel, Glut. They are easy to say, have no trademark risk as model names, and give a series you can extend (Bise, Gletscher, Moräne, Mutt, Schrattenkalk…).
