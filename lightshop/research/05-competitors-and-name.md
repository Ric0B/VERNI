## What it means for you

1. **The woven, non-planar look will spread.** Claywoven sells the method for $8 a month, and MakerWorld has free non-planar lamp files. The look alone won't protect you for long. What hobbyists can't easily copy: a finished, certified lamp with Swiss electrics, made to measure and delivered in a week. Sell that, and show the process (time-lapses) as proof.
2. **outil.li proves that colour and translucency sell at €399.** Their Jello has 4 colour combinations, and several were sold out. Your configurator offers 5 forms × 3 sizes × 7 colours plus two-tone, priced live. That's more choice at a lower price.
3. **The dimmer is a product.** outil.li charges about €200 extra for its dial. Your CHF 29 cord dimmer is the cheap option. A designed table dimmer (printed housing, certified inline dimmer inside) could later sell for CHF 89–129.
4. **B2B works the way outil.li does it:** a catalogue, technical specs, and modules for architects (Bolha). You already have the sales sheet (`b2b/`). Add a spec PDF per lamp (E27, W, lm, K, materials, fire rating) and a "joint development" offer for hotels and restaurants.
5. **Circular gets you press.** outil.li says "designed to come back round" but shows no take-back scheme. You can make it concrete: send back an old shade and get CHF 30 off a new one; the PLA gets shredded or goes to a recycler. That's cheap to run and a strong story for social media.
6. **Swiss is your edge against all of them:** prices in CHF, no customs, a Swiss plug, delivery in 5–8 days and repairs in Switzerland. outil.li ships from the Netherlands and Gantri from the US.

## Recommended price changes

- Keep the configurator prices for launch (S CHF 139–189, M 169–229, L 199–269).
- After 20 sales, raise the L sizes by CHF 20–30. Large lamps are the statement pieces, and outil.li shows the room above.
- Korb (woven) is your most unusual lamp and costs the least material (about 26 g). Leave it at CHF 219 for M. Don't discount it.
- An "Atelier" line (one-offs, editions with a certificate) at CHF 390–690 sits right next to outil.li.

## The name "centerforphotons"

**What I found:** no lamp brand, shop or company called "Center for Photons" or "centerforphotons" shows up in web search; the results bring up only physics institutes. I couldn't check domains or Instagram handles from this session.

| | |
|---|---|
| ➕ Memorable, ironic and conceptual: a lamp "centre" that makes photons. It suits the experimental line (woven lamps, vase-mode concepts, one-offs) | ➖ In Switzerland it sounds like the **PSI Center for Photon Science** in Villigen, the national lab behind the Swiss Light Source and SwissFEL ([PSI](https://www.psi.ch/en/cps)). Many readers will think of a research institute, not a lamp shop |
| ➕ International: works in English, for Instagram and press | ➖ Searches for "center for photons" return physics institutes ("Center for Photonics", PSI). Weak SEO for the shop |
| ➕ No direct lamp competitor found with this name | ➖ US spelling "Center", while British, Swiss and EU English use "Centre". In German it's awkward to say and to spell on the phone |
| | ➖ Says nothing about 3D printing, design or Switzerland. The short form "CfP" usually means "call for papers" |
| | ➖ "Photon" alone is a registered trademark of Anycubic for 3D printers ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/883/13/photon-88313407.html)). That's a different class from lamps, but the same 3D-printing audience |

**My recommendation:** keep **Schichtlicht** (or another German-Swiss name from report 4) for the shop. Use **Center for Photons** as the name of your experimental line or studio: "Schichtlicht · Center for Photons" for the woven and conceptual pieces, the one-offs and an Instagram account that shows the process. That keeps its art-lab charm without the search and confusion problems in the shop.

If you want it as the main brand, a Swiss twist makes it stronger and more your own, for example **"Amt für Photonen"** ("Office for Photons", a nod to Swiss officialdom). Run the same checks first.

**Checks before you decide (15 minutes):**
1. Domains: centerforphotons.com / .ch, centreforphotons.com, at [nic.ch](https://www.nic.ch) or a registrar.
2. Handles: @centerforphotons on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.
3. Trademark: [Swissreg](https://www.swissreg.ch) and [EUIPO eSearch](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/), class 11 (lighting), for "Center for Photons" and "Photons".
4. Company register: [zefix.ch](https://www.zefix.ch).
5. Ask 5 people in Switzerland what kind of business "Center for Photons" is. If most say "a research lab", use it only for the line.
