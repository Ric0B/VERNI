**How the market splits:**
1. **Under CHF 70:** crowded and price-driven (Etsy, 3dlampe, 3D-MST). Your 15 hours a week can't win on volume.
2. **CHF 90–200:** thin in Switzerland. Austria and Germany have a few shops, but most look homemade. **This is your gap.**
3. **CHF 200–400:** proven by Dehgraf, Gantri and MGX. It needs real design quality, great photos and press.

## Recommendation: "accessible premium", mostly CHF 129–249

Don't go purely high-end or purely mid-range. Build the brand at **CHF 149–249** and add a few entry products at **CHF 69–99** to get first-time buyers and gifts.

- Swiss customers spend about 1.5× what German customers spend on the same thing. The brand promise is "Swiss-made, considered, safe".
- Material costs stay about CHF 30–55 at any price (see the costs below), so margins at CHF 149+ are 60–75%.
- Your time is the bottleneck, not printer capacity. Fewer, more expensive orders beat many cheap ones.
- B2B (cafés, hotels, offices, architects) is the bigger win. One fit-out of 20 pendants at CHF 180 = CHF 3,600 in a single order. Put a B2B page on the site from day one.

## What's in demand (trends for 2026)

1. **Cordless / rechargeable table lamps.** This is the strongest trend in table lamps. Market reports estimate 5–8% growth a year ([GMI](https://www.gii.tw/report/gmi2027530-desk-lamp-market-opportunity-growth-drivers.html), [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/cordless-rechargeable-table-lamp-market)). Cordless models already make up more than half of desk-lamp sales by one estimate. They suit restaurants, balconies and gifts. Build them on 5 V USB-C (power outside the low-voltage rules), using a certified kit.
2. **Organic and wavy shapes:** ribbed "pleated" shades, mushroom silhouettes, a hand-made feel. The fluted vase-mode look prints beautifully on Bambu machines.
3. **Warm, dimmable 2200–2700 K light.** People buy atmosphere, not brightness.
4. **Personalisation:** colour choice, initials and engravings (as at Wellmade). A good premium upsell (+CHF 20–30).
5. **Sustainability:** bio-based PLA, repairable, spare shades, "made to order in Switzerland". Don't overclaim: PLA only composts industrially.
6. **Modularity:** swap shades on the same base (VNEN). Sell extra shades for CHF 59–79. Very good for repeat purchases.

## Capacity and revenue

- **Printers:** about 2 shades a day per printer (6–10 h each, overnight), so up to about 100 lamps a month with 2 printers. Printers aren't the bottleneck.
- **Your 15 h a week:** about 5 h goes to content and marketing, 3 h to admin, packing and customer service, which leaves about 7 h for assembly and QC. That's **15–25 lamps a week at most**, realistically 40–60 a month after the first year.
- **Scenarios (average order CHF 175):**

## Paid ads: is it worth it?

- Meta (Instagram/Facebook) home decor CPMs: about USD 8–14, rising about 20% a year, and **Q4 is 25–50% more expensive** ([sovran](https://sovran.ai/benchmarks/meta-ads-cpm-by-industry), [TGM](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/?p=44251)). Expect higher costs in Switzerland: about CHF 10–18 per 1,000 impressions.
- Typical ROAS (revenue per franc spent on ads) for small direct-to-consumer brands is 1.5–2.5 ([Triple Whale via influee](https://influee.co/ch-fr/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks)).
- **Your break-even ROAS:** at about 70% margin before ads and about 3% fees, each franc of ads must bring in **about CHF 1.50** of sales to break even. That's very achievable for a product this visual. So **ads make sense for you**, once a few things are in place:
  1. Have at least 20 organic sales and 10 reviews.
  2. Have 6–10 strong short videos made by you (a time-lapse of the print, then the lamp switched on).
  3. Then test **CHF 10–15 a day for 3 weeks (about CHF 300–450)** on Meta (Advantage+ Shopping), targeting CH and the German-speaking region.
  4. Scale by +20% a week while ROAS stays above 2.5. Stop below 1.5.
- **Don't run ads before Christmas 2026.** You won't be ready and Q4 is expensive. Test from **February–March 2027**.
- Google Shopping: a free listing plus a small paid budget (CHF 5 a day) for "Designerlampe 3D Druck" and "Tischlampe kabellos" searches once you have reviews.
