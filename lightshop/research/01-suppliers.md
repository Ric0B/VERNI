**How to buy:** pick 2–3 hero colours (natural/frosted, warm white, sand, stone grey). Order 10–20 kg refills at a time to get bulk or free-shipping prices. Dry translucent filament before printing (about 55 °C for 6 h), or the shades go milky and uneven.

**Material per lamp:** a 25 cm table lamp shade printed in vase mode uses about 150–300 g; with a printed base, about 400–700 g. That is **CHF 4–15 per lamp** in filament.

### Heat: what decides PLA or PETG

- PLA softens at about 55–60 °C ([JLC3DP](https://jlc3dp.com/blog/petg-temperature-resistance)). An LED inside a closed shade can push the air to 50 °C or more ([QIDI](https://qidi3d.com/blogs/print-lab/translucent-filaments-heat-safe-lampshades)).
- PETG handles about 65–75 °C. ASA and PC go higher.
- **Rules for the brand:** LED only, at most 5–7 W. Keep at least 20–30 mm between bulb and shade, plus vent openings top and bottom. Print the part that holds the socket in PETG or ASA, even when the shade is PLA. Then **measure it**: run each design for 4 h at the highest wattage you'll allow, take the wall temperature with a cheap IR thermometer, and record the result. That log is part of your technical file (see legal notes).

## Electrical parts (buy only pre-certified)

The safest setup is a **Class II (double-insulated) lamp with a Europlug (Type C)** cord set. A Europlug fits Swiss Type 13 sockets as well as German and Austrian ones, so one cord SKU covers your whole market. Only switch to a Swiss Type 11 plug if a buyer asks.

## Shipping (Swiss Post, 2026)

- PostPac Economy up to 2 kg: **CHF 9.00** list price from 1 Jan 2026, minus a CHF 1.50 online discount (CHF 2.00 for business customers until 30 Jun 2026) ([Post](https://www.post.ch/en/about-us/news/2025/new-prices-for-economy-parcels-and-international-consignments-from-1-january-2026)). Priority prices didn't change.
- Open a free business account (Post "My Post Business") and create labels online.
- **Offer free shipping in CH above CHF 120.** Your average order will be above that, so put shipping into the price.
