# Risk assessment: printed lamps (table, floor, pendant, cordless)

| # | Hazard | Cause | Design measure | Check | Remaining risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Electric shock | Access to live parts, damaged insulation | Class II certified cord set; socket fully enclosed by PETG mount; no metal on the outside; no user access to terminals | Test finger (pencil) cannot reach terminals | Low |
| 2 | Cord pulled out of the socket | Pulling the lamp by its cord | Strain relief at the socket (M10 nipple clamp) and cable channel in the base | 6 kg pull test | Low |
| 3 | Shade deforms / fire | Heat from the bulb, PLA softens from 55 °C | LED only, max. 7 W label; 30 mm minimum clearance; vents top and bottom; PETG at the socket | 4-hour temperature test, shade below 45 °C | Low. Warning in instructions: "LED only, max. 7 W" |
| 4 | Customer fits an incandescent or halogen bulb | Misuse | Label + instructions + included LED bulb; E27 halogen still possible | — | Medium → mitigated by warnings; consider E27 sockets with LED-only marking |
| 5 | Lamp tips over | Light shade, heavy bulb at the top | Steel ballast in the base, low centre of gravity | 6° tilt test (table), 10° (floor) | Low |
| 6 | Battery fire (Föhn, Glut) | Damaged or overcharged lithium cell | Only kits with protected cells and documented UN 38.3 / EN 62133; no own battery assembly | Supplier documents | Low |
| 7 | Sun damage behind glass | UV and heat soften PLA | Instructions: not in direct sun behind glass | — | Low, cosmetic |
| 8 | Children | Small parts, battery access | Battery behind screws; no small detachable parts | Visual | Low |
| 9 | Pendant falls | Wrong installation | Installation by an electrician (instructions); certified canopy kit rated for the weight | Weight under 1 kg | Low |
